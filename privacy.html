Privacy policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Lia (home server) is a private assistant used only by the household that runs it. When the account owner signs in with Google, Lia can use Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Sheets and Google Contacts data to help that household. It uses only the permissions the owner granted.

  • Google data is processed only on the household's own server, to do the tasks the owner asks for.
  • It is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with anyone outside the household, except the AI service that runs the assistant, which processes it to answer each request.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored only on that server.
  • The owner can remove Lia's access at any time in their Google Account, under Security, then Third-party connections.

Lia's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
